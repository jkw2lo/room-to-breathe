How to play

Make the flat fit, flow and feel right.

Every plan comes with a moving-van's worth of furniture. Get all of it in, then fine-tune the arrangement.

  1. Fit every piece. Furniture can't overlap walls, doorways or the front door's swing.
  2. Flow. A person needs a 75 cm path from the front door to every room, bed side, sofa and appliance.
  3. Harmony. Feng shui and good design: beds against solid walls and out of the door line, the sofa facing the TV, desks that see the door, plants by windows, every room lit.

We ran an optimiser on each plan to find a strong designer layout. Score within 90% of it for three stars.

Three ways to play. Puzzle is the relaxed mode. Moving Day is against the clock: the movers bring pieces one at a time, and if you're too slow they dump them by the front door. Sandbox gives you the whole 70-piece library to design your own home, with feedback but no rules.

Most pieces come in several styles (king, platform or canopy beds; round or bench dining; Chesterfield sofas…). Select a placed piece and pick one from the Style menu.
